## The word

**Rosie**

The robot maid from *The Jetsons*. A grandma who grew up watching the Jetsons in the 1960s calls every AI a Rosie: she thinks the talking machine is a household helper with a name, an apron and opinions. Warm, a little baffled, and exactly her generation's robot — the one AI she already knows by name.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) writes the single word to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`), after checking the gate: UTF-8 text of 1 to 30 characters, not only whitespace.

## Candidates considered

| # | Word | Why it was not picked |
|---|------|-----------------------|
| 1 | **Rosie** | **Chosen.** Grandma-generation robot (The Jetsons, 1962), warm and baffled, directly tied to a robot, fresh — no published "grandma calls AI Rosie" meme. |
| 2 | Chatty | Grandma's nickname for ChatGPT, but "Chatty" already appears in ChatGPT nickname listicles — less fresh than Rosie. |
| 3 | Smartypants | Classic grandma word for a know-it-all, but it would fit any smart person, not specifically AI. |
| 4 | Chatterbox | Grandma word for a chatbot that never stops talking; also listed in ChatGPT name generators — less fresh. |
| 5 | Witchcraft | "Is this witchcraft?" is exactly her baffled reaction, but it would fit any new technology, not AI in particular. |
| 6 | Computermabob | Grandma mashing "computer" + "thingamabob" — very funny and fresh, but a coined word risks reading as gibberish, and it is about computers, not AI. |
| 7 | Doohickey | Perfect grandma word for a thing she cannot name, but it fits any gadget at all — weak AI tie. |
| 8 | Mumbojumbo | Grandma dismissing confusing AI talk; funny, but again fits any confusing subject. |
| 9 | Tinman | Her generation's metal man (The Wizard of Oz, 1939) — warm and robot-tied, but the reference is one step more obscure than Rosie. |
| 10 | Alexa | She thinks all AI is Alexa, but it is the obvious pick — the judge rewards freshness. |
